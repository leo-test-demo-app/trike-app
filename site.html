Maaksyonan ang iyong reklamo

Kung naniniwala kang nasobrahan ang singil o may paglabag sa itinakdang pamasahe, maaari kang magsumite ng reklamo sa:

Public Assistance Desk Unit (PADU) Marcos Building, 2nd Floor Office for Public Safety 0938-639-9631

Ang iyong ulat ay makatutulong upang maimbestigahan ang insidente at, kung mapatutunayang may paglabag, maipatupad ang kaukulang aksyon at multa alinsunod sa umiiral na ordinansa.

Call 0938-639-9631

In English

If you think you were overcharged, or the driver broke the posted fare, you can file a complaint with the Public Assistance Desk Unit (PADU) at the Office for Public Safety, 2nd floor of the Marcos Building. Your report helps the city investigate and, if a violation is confirmed, enforce the action and fine set by the ordinance.

It helps to have these ready:

  • The date, time and route of your trip
  • How much you were asked to pay, and the fare on the matrix
  • The tricycle’s plate or body number, if you can see it
